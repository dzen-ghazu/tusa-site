# Welcome to TUSA Docs

swagat! TUSA Docs is the central hub for the documentation that helps students and club leaders use the TUSA platform. Whether you are starting a club, managing one, joining activities, attending events, or volunteering, this site brings the key guides together in one place.

## Choose your pathway

### For club leaders and committee members

Use the Club Admin Guide to set up and run a club from the ground up. It covers the full process from starting a club, through reaffiliation, to running a store, managing memberships, people, and events.

- [Club Admin Guide](club-admin-guide/index.md)
- [Getting Started](club-admin-guide/1-getting-started/index.md)
- [Your Store](club-admin-guide/3-your-store/index.md)
- [Your Events](club-admin-guide/6-your-events/index.md)

### For students

Use the Student Help Centre to find out how to participate in student life at TUSA. It covers joining clubs, starting a new club, finding and attending events, understanding compliance forms, and volunteering.

- [Student Help Centre](student-help-centre/index.md)
- [Joining Clubs](student-help-centre/1-clubs-and-societies/1-join-clubs.md)
- [Finding and Attending Events](student-help-centre/2-events/1-attend-events.md)
- [Food Hub Volunteering](student-help-centre/3-volunteering/1-food-hub-volunteering.md)

## Recommended starting points

- New club admin? Start with [Start Your Club](club-admin-guide/1-getting-started/1-start-your-club.md) and then move through the rest of the Getting Started section.
- Need to manage club payments, store operations, or memberships? Visit [Your Store](club-admin-guide/3-your-store/index.md) and [Your Memberships](club-admin-guide/4-your-memberships/index.md).
- Want to get involved as a student? Begin with [Joining Clubs](student-help-centre/1-clubs-and-societies/1-join-clubs.md) or [Finding and Attending Events](student-help-centre/2-events/1-attend-events.md).

## What this site covers

- Club setup, affiliation, and ongoing administration
- Store products, orders, refunds, and delivery
- Membership products, rules, and access control
- Club member and store staff management
- Event creation, approvals, and check-in
- Student participation, event attendance, compliance, and volunteering
